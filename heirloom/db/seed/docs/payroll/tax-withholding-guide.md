# Tax Withholding Guide

Owner: Payroll · Entry type: regulation

## Federal income tax

PayCore calculates federal income tax using the employee's current W-4 elections and the IRS percentage method tables for the pay frequency.

## Social Security and Medicare

Social Security is withheld at 6.2% up to the annual wage base. Medicare is withheld at 1.45% on all wages, plus an additional 0.9% on wages over $200,000 in the calendar year.

## Pre-tax deductions

Section 125 deductions (medical, dental, vision, FSA, HSA through payroll) reduce wages for federal income tax, Social Security, and Medicare. Pre-tax 401(k) reduces wages for federal income tax only.

## Supplemental wages

Bonuses and retro earnings paid separately may use the 22% flat supplemental rate.

## State and local

State withholding uses the work location's state rules. Reciprocity agreements are applied when the employee lives and works in different states.
