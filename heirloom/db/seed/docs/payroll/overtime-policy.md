# Overtime Policy

Owner: Payroll · Effective: January 2026

## Purpose

This policy defines how Payroll calculates overtime pay for non-exempt employees.

## Workweek

The workweek is a fixed, recurring period of 168 hours, Sunday 12:00 a.m. through Saturday 11:59 p.m., unless a client configures a different start day.

## Calculation

Overtime is calculated weekly. Each workweek stands alone: hours are never averaged across two weeks, even when the pay period is biweekly. An employee who works 50 hours in week one and 30 hours in week two of a biweekly period is owed 10 hours of overtime, even though total hours equal 80.

Overtime is paid at 1.5 times the regular rate for hours worked over 40 in the workweek.

## Hours worked

Hours worked include all time the employee is required to be on duty. Paid time off, holidays, and sick time are not hours worked and do not count toward the 40-hour threshold.

## State rules

Some states require daily overtime (for example, over 8 hours in a day). PayCore applies state daily overtime rules when the client's work location requires it. Daily and weekly overtime are not paid twice for the same hour.

## Source of hours

Payroll uses the approved hours delivered by ClockLine. ClockLine identifies which hours are overtime; Payroll applies the rate. If ClockLine and PayCore disagree on overtime hours, PayCore uses ClockLine's hours and raises an exception.
