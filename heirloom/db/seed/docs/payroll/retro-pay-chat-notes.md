# Retro pay questions (copied from the #payroll-help channel)

These are informal notes copied from team chat. They are not reviewed documentation.

---

**Mar 4** · Question from the support desk: a client's rate increase was entered late and is now effective three pay periods back. Do we redo overtime too?

**Maria Lopez:** Yes. When a rate change is backdated, the overtime premium for every affected week has to be recalculated at the new regular rate. People forget this. I run the retro calc week by week, not for the whole period at once.

---

**May 19** · Question from Raj: why don't we just compute the retro amount as rate difference times total hours?

**Maria Lopez:** Because overtime hours need the 1.5x premium applied to the difference. Rate difference times total hours underpays anyone with overtime in those weeks. I handle those manually in the retro worksheet.

---

**Jul 22** · Question from a new analyst: where is the retro pay process written down?

**Maria Lopez:** It isn't, honestly. Ask me and I'll walk you through it. I keep meaning to write it up.

---

**Aug 30** · Question from the support desk: retro correction for a timesheet that was fixed after payroll closed. Overtime changed from 2 to 6 hours.

**Maria Lopez:** Pay the 4 extra overtime hours in the next regular run as a retro earning, coded RETRO-OT so it shows separately on the pay statement. Don't reopen the closed period.
