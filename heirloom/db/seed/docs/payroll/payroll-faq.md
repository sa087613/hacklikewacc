# Payroll FAQ

Note: some answers below have not been reviewed since 2024.

**When is unused PTO paid out after an employee leaves?**
Unused PTO is paid on the employee's final paycheck, issued in an off-cycle run on the last day of work.

**Can an employee change their direct deposit mid-period?**
Yes. Changes made before cutoff apply to the current period.

**Why does an employee's overtime look different on their pay statement than in ClockLine?**
PayCore shows overtime pay, not overtime hours. Divide by 1.5 times the regular rate to compare.

**Do we still support paper checks?**
Yes, but they are being phased out in 2024. (Outdated: paper checks are still supported as of 2026.)

**How do I correct last period's overtime?**
Contact the payroll help channel.

**What is the cutoff time?**
Cutoff is 3:00 p.m. Eastern. (Outdated: cutoff moved to 5:00 p.m. Eastern. See the Pay Schedule Guide.)
