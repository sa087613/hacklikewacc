# Pay Schedule Guide

Owner: Ana Torres

## Supported frequencies

- Weekly: 52 pay dates per year
- Biweekly: 26 pay dates per year (most common)
- Semimonthly: 24 pay dates per year, on the 15th and last day of the month

## Cutoffs

Payroll cutoff is 5:00 p.m. Eastern, two business days before the pay date. ClockLine sends the final approved hours file at cutoff. Changes after cutoff go into the next pay period.

## Holidays

If a pay date falls on a bank holiday, the pay date moves to the previous business day and cutoff moves one business day earlier.

## Off-cycle runs

Off-cycle runs are allowed for terminations and corrections. They require approval from a payroll lead.
