# Payroll Product Roadmap (FY27)

Owner: Raj Patel · Updated: October 2026

## Q1 2027

- **Build an overtime calculator in PayCore.** Today PayCore takes overtime hours from ClockLine and only applies the rate. We plan to build our own overtime calculator so PayCore can compute weekly and daily overtime from raw hours, handle state rules, and stop depending on ClockLine's overtime split. Estimate: 3 sprints.
- Pay statement redesign
