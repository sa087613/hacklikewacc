# COBRA Continuation Process

## Trigger

A qualifying event: termination, reduction in hours that causes loss of eligibility, or loss of dependent status.

## Notice

BenHub sends the election notice within 14 days of being notified of the qualifying event.

## Election

The individual has 60 days to elect continuation. Coverage is retroactive to the loss date if elected.
