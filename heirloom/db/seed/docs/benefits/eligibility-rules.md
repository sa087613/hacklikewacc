# Benefits Eligibility Rules

Owner: Priya Nair · Maintained by: Marcus Hill · System: BenHub

## 1. Who is eligible

An employee is eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage when they are a full-time employee, or a part-time employee who meets the hours threshold in section 2.

## 2. Hours threshold

### 2.1 Measurement

Eligibility hours are measured over each biweekly period. An employee meets the threshold when they have 60 or more eligible hours in the biweekly period, which equals an average of 30 hours per week. BenHub assumes all hours arrive as biweekly totals; it does not look at individual workweeks.

### 2.2 Which hours count

Benefits eligibility uses scheduled hours, not overtime hours. Overtime hours are excluded from the eligibility calculation so that a temporary overtime spike does not make an employee eligible and then ineligible again. Paid time off counts as eligible hours.

### 2.3 Look-back

Variable-hour employees are measured over a 12-month standard measurement period. If the average is 30 or more hours per week, coverage is offered for the following 12-month stability period.

## 3. Waiting period

Coverage begins on the first of the month after 60 days of employment.

## 4. Loss of eligibility

If an employee drops below the threshold for two consecutive biweekly periods and is not in a stability period, coverage ends at the end of the month. The employee is offered COBRA.

## 5. Inputs

BenHub receives hours from ClockLine every two weeks as a biweekly total per employee, split into scheduled hours and overtime hours.
