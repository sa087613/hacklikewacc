# Benefits vendor contract terms

Vendor: Bellwether Health Administrators (fictional) · Owner: Priya Nair · Confidential: Benefits team only

## Term

Three-year agreement, renewing January 2027. Either party may terminate with 180 days' notice.

## Pricing

Per-employee-per-month administration fee of $4.10, with a volume discount tier above 50,000 covered lives.

## Service levels

Enrollment files processed within 2 business days. Late processing credits of 5% of the monthly fee per missed day.

## Data handling

The vendor receives enrollment elections only. No payroll amounts are shared.
