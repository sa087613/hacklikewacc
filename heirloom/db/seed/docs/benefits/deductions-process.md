# Pre-tax Deductions Process

Owner: Priya Nair

## Overview

Benefits sends each employee's elections to Payroll every pay period. Payroll deducts the per-period amount from gross pay.

## How amounts are calculated

The per-period deduction is the annual employee premium divided by the number of pay periods in the year. The deduction amount does not change with hours worked or overtime.

## Timing

Elections approved in BenHub before payroll cutoff are sent in that period's file. Later changes start the following period. Missed deductions are caught up over the next two periods, not in a single period.

## Eligibility changes

If an employee loses eligibility, BenHub sends a stop record, and deductions end with the last pay date in the coverage month.
