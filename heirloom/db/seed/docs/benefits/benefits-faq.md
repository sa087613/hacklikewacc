# Benefits FAQ

**Does overtime make someone eligible for benefits?**
No. Eligibility uses scheduled hours, not overtime. See Benefits Eligibility Rules.

**Do deductions go up when an employee works overtime?**
No. Deduction amounts are fixed per pay period.

**When is open enrollment?**
October 15–31. (Outdated: open enrollment moved to November 1–15 in 2025.)
