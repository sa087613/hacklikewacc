# Open Enrollment Guide

Owner: Priya Nair

## Window

Open enrollment runs November 1–15 each year. Elections take effect January 1.

## What employees can change

Medical, dental, and vision plans; dependents; FSA and HSA contributions.

## After the window

Changes outside open enrollment require a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, loss of other coverage) reported within 30 days.

## Support

Lena Park coordinates the enrollment readiness checklist. Escalations go to Priya Nair.
