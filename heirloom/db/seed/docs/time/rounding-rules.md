# Punch Rounding Rules

Owner: Sam Okafor

## Current rule

ClockLine rounds each punch to the nearest 6 minutes (one tenth of an hour). A punch at 8:02 rounds to 8:00; a punch at 8:04 rounds to 8:06.

## Neutrality

Rounding must be neutral over time: it cannot consistently favor the employer.

## Effect on overtime

Rounded punches are the hours used for the overtime calculator and for the hours files sent to Payroll and Benefits. Any change to the rounding interval changes overtime hours downstream.

## Proposed change (under discussion)

Product has proposed rounding to the nearest 15 minutes instead of 6, to match a large client's policy. No decision yet.
