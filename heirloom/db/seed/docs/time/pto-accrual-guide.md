# PTO Accrual Guide

## Accrual rate

Default accrual is 1 hour of PTO per 30 hours worked. Clients can configure tiers by tenure.

## Cap

Accrual stops when the balance reaches the cap (default 120 hours) and resumes when the balance drops below it.

## Overtime

Overtime hours accrue PTO at the same rate as regular hours.
