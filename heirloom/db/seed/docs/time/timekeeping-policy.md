# Timekeeping Policy

## Punches

Employees punch in and out through the ClockLine app, web clock, or a physical terminal. Meal breaks of 30 minutes or more are unpaid and require punches.

## Timesheet approval

Managers approve timesheets by payroll cutoff. Unapproved time is not sent to Payroll.

## Edits

Edits after approval require manager re-approval and create an audit record.
